Stress-Tolerant Seed Varieties of India

A reference catalogue by Precision Development

Source

Reference catalogue of — stress-tolerant seed varieties released in India (Central Sub-Committee on Crop Standards, Notification and Release of Varieties), each matched to its Seednet (Government of India) record. Every row links to its official sources, the developing institution and its contact details, published research (Google Scholar, OpenAlex) and an evidence-based reading of drought, heat, salinity, flood and submergence tolerance.

Open a row for the full evidence. Research summaries were produced by language models from web searches (Aug 2025 and Oct 2026) and always cite their sources; treat them as leads to verify, not as official data.
